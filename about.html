About

A small company doing one big thing.

Solar Inference is a small New Jersey company that rents GPU servers to AI teams from a home server room, and our next step is the biggest one yet, a full-size AI computer of our own.

Who we are

We started at home, and that is where we are staying.

We began by running GPU servers in our own house and renting them to AI teams online, and along the way we learned how much electrical capacity most homes leave unused, at the very moment the largest AI companies are waiting years for the grid to catch up with them.

So we are building an AI computer of our own, and once it has proven the design, we plan to place the next ones with a couple of other homes.

How we work

A few things we hold to.

We own and run every machine.

Hosts never buy or run anything, since the hardware, power, internet, insurance and upkeep are ours.

A person makes the calls that matter.

AI agents help us build and run the monitoring, pricing and power control around the clock, and a person decides anything that matters.

We never look at our customers’ work.

Each computer has its own fiber line and never joins a home’s network, and what customers run on it is theirs.

The rent is fixed.

It doesn’t rise or fall with rentals, since GPU rental prices swing and that risk is ours to carry.

We ask the town first.

If a town’s code is silent we ask for a ruling, and if a town has banned data centers we won’t place an AI computer there.

Small until it’s proven.

We are proving this out on a small number of homes, and we would much rather get a few right than rush into many.

For compute customers

Need compute?

Our machines are listed on online GPU marketplaces for rent by the hour, and for longer or larger work we contract directly, so please write to us with what you need to run and for how long.
