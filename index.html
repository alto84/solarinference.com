Bringing AI computing home.

We are building a full data center server into a home, one AI computer with its own power, cooling, battery and fiber line, and once our own is running, we plan to place the next ones with a couple of other homes (the home, for its part, gets a fixed monthly rent, heat in the winter and backup power when the grid goes down).

The AI computer

A real data center server, at home.

Power and the utility

Why homes

The short answer is electricity.

Chips are hard enough to come by, but the real wait is for power, since a new data center can spend years in line for a grid connection while the average American home uses well under half of the electrical service it already has.

Run with AI agents

AI agents help us build and run our monitoring, pricing and power control around the clock, and a person makes the calls that matter.

Lessons from home

Power, heat and noise all behave differently in a house than in a data center, and we would rather learn those lessons at home scale before building anything bigger.
